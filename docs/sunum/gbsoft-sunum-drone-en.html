City Vision · Problem / 17

Municipalities often learn about urban problems too late.

Citizen reports and human field inspections are valuable, but repeatedly scanning a large area at the same frequency, angle and evidentiary standard is difficult.

Visibility gap

Unreported problems do not enter the record

Quiet neighborhoods and hard-to-reach areas are inspected less frequently.

Repeatability gap

The same location is not captured to the same standard

Measuring change requires repeating the same route, altitude and camera angle.

Operational burden

Inspection depends on staff availability

Field preparation, travel and data transfer limit the number of missions.

DAMAGE NOT DETECTED EARLY
Outcome
The problem grows, increasing repair cost and response time.

City Vision does not replace human inspection; it strengthens it with prioritized, geolocated and comparable evidence.
